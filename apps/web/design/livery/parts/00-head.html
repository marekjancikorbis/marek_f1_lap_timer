Purple Sector, painted as a livery

A redesign proposal for every screen of the app. Paddock screens sit on the light bodywork; the drive screens are the navy panel. Every lap wears its own stripe: its three sector results as slanted bands, from the feed card up to the full-screen HUD. These are design pages only. Nothing in the app has changed, and all drivers, times and routes shown are sample data.

The system

Saira for everything: times in its condensed italic, interface text at normal width. Navy is the colour you press on #f7f9fc; white is the colour you press on navy. Purple, green and yellow keep their timing meanings and always appear with a label.
