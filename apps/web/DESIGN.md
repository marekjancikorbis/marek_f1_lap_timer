# Purple Sector design: Glass tiles (current)

Since 2026-11-08 the app wears **Glass tiles** (mockups: `design/redesigns/`, the "Glass tiles" pages). It is a layer
over Livery, not a rewrite: `src/styles/glass.css` (loaded last) re-points the Livery tokens below at the active theme.
- **Themes:** `src/styles/themes.css` + `src/lib/themes.ts`, generated by `design/redesigns/tools/app_themes.py` from the
  mockup palettes (Mono is the default). Picked in Settings, stored in localStorage, applied before first paint (`src/lib/theme.ts`).
- **Surfaces:** cards are tinted glass without blur (over a soft gradient blur is invisible and costs frames); floating
  chrome (tab bar, sheets, toasts, map overlays) has a real blur. Radii: cards 28, sheets 32+, controls pills.
- **Background:** the `.bg` layer in `index.html`, three colour spots orbiting with transform only (compositor).
- **Maps:** Mapbox dark style; routes white on a dark casing; colours only where laps are compared.
- **Sector colours** are the same in every theme (purple #9d5cff, green #1fd27a, yellow #ffbe1a). Type is the system
  font, upright, sentence case.
- The Livery rules below still describe the token names and the structure; where they disagree with the above, Glass tiles wins.

# Purple Sector design: Livery

The app is painted like a race car. Mockups of every screen: `design/livery/index.html` (open it in a browser; it is assembled from `design/livery/parts/*.html` with `cat parts/*.html > index.html`). The previous blue-black design is git tag `design-blue-v1`.

## Rules

- **Two sides of the car.** Paddock screens (feed, routes, profiles, review) sit on the light body. Everything from Start to the end of a drive is navy with off-white numerals.
- **Every lap wears its stripe.** A lap's three sector results are three slanted bands (purple / green / yellow by the PLAN.md section 9 rules). Large bands carry S1/S2/S3 or the gap; a sector without a time is an empty outline.
- **The brand stripe** (purple, green, yellow, slanted, always in that order) appears only on navy panels, never under data.
- **Action colour.** Navy is what you press on the light body; off-white is what you press on navy.
- **Never pure white `#fff` or pure black `#000`**, including shadows, scrims and SVG fills.
- **Drivers are roundels:** initials in the avatar colour inside an off-white ring, or the photo.
- Stage (point-to-point) routes have no sessions: one run, one summary. Sessions of laps exist only on lap routes.
